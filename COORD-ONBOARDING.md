# NJY App — Coordinator Guide (One page)

Hare Krsna 🙏 This is the guide for **NJY Coordinators** — the people who
speak with chanters every day. If you can read this, you can use the app.

## Getting the app on your phone

1. Open **Chrome** on your Android and go to: **https://njy-7jn.pages.dev**
2. Sign in with the username + password Prabhuji sent you
3. Chrome will show a small **"Install app"** button in the address bar
   — tap it. Or use the top nav's **Install app** pill.
4. NJY appears on your home screen like a real app. Open it from there.

## Janmashtami day — adding chanters

Every person you sign up for chanting goes through the **Janmashtami** tab.

1. Tap **Janmashtami** in the top row of tabs.
2. Fill **Name**, **Mobile** (10 digits, no +91 needed), **Pincode**.
3. Tap **Add ↵**. Their serial number is auto-assigned to your range.
4. All the entries you make today appear at the bottom, newest first.

**Have a big list?** In the same tab, use the **Upload Excel or CSV file**
card. Download the template first, fill it in Excel, save, upload. Or paste
rows straight from Excel into the "Paste from Excel" box below.

## Every day after — marking who chanted

Tap **My roll** in the top row. Each chanter is a row with:

| What | What it does |
|---|---|
| ⚪ Circle | Tap once = you contacted them (yellow). Tap again = they responded (orange). Third tap resets. |
| Dropdown (chanter → daily → …) | Once the person commits to daily chanting, change to **daily**. |
| ✓ chanted | Tap when the person tells you they chanted today. Turns green. |
| WhatsApp button | Opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled Tamil + English message to that person. |
| 📅 button | Shows their last 14 days. Tap any past day to backfill if you forgot to mark it. |

## The colors mean

- ⚪ **White** — fresh, nothing marked today
- 🟡 **Yellow** — you contacted them today
- 🟠 **Orange** — they responded today
- 🟢 **Green** — they chanted today
- 🔴 **Red** — daily chanter but no chant in 3+ days. Needs attention.

The colors reset each morning at midnight.

## Points and leaderboard

At the top of the app, near your name, you see a **🪙 coin chip** — that's
your points today and overall. Tap it to see your profile: exactly how you
earned each point.

The **Leaderboard** tab shows how you rank against other coordinators. The
top 3 get medals. On the right side of the screen (on laptop/desktop) you
see a live leaderboard that updates every 45 seconds.

**How points work** — every action counts:
- Marking a chanter as chanted today: **+10**
- Contacting a chanter (yellow tap): **+5**
- Someone attending an NJY event: **+50**
- All your chanters chanting today (perfect day): **+50 bonus**
- 7-day chanting streak per chanter: **+20**
- 30-day chanting streak per chanter: **+50**

For the full list, tap **How points work** on the leaderboard.

## If something breaks

- **Blank page or stuck?** — pull down to refresh, or close and reopen the app
- **"Wrong password"?** — password is exactly what Prabhuji sent, all lowercase, no spaces
- **Can't add a chanter, "range exhausted"?** — you've filled your first 100 slots. Ask Prabhuji to open a second block.
- **Anything else?** — screenshot and send to Prabhuji or Varsha.
